About

Who I Am

I'm a 3D artist and aspiring indie game developer currently studying Animación 3D, Juegos y Entornos Interactivos in Pamplona. My background covers systems administration and web development, but it's Blender where I spend most of my time — modeling, lighting, shading, compositing.

Before this I completed a Grado Medio in Sistemas Microinformáticos y Redes, which means I can set up the server and render the scene. I've done internships doing web development and video editing for real estate, which taught me that deadlines and client taste are their own kind of constraint.

I want to ship original indie games — where every art direction call, gameplay mechanic, and design decision comes from the same pair of hands. Raw, authored, intentional.
