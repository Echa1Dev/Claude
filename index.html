Who I Am

Javier Echaide is a 3D artist and indie game developer based in Spain. He works primarily in Blender — modeling detailed characters and environments, crafting node-based materials, building animations, and compositing full scenes from scratch. His work lives at the intersection of art and code: from hand-sculpted 3D assets to browser-based interactive experiences, he builds the full stack of a digital world.

The goal is simple and enormous: build original indie games. Worlds that didn't exist before — designed, modeled, coded, and shipped by one person. Not a job, not a client brief. Something that matters.
