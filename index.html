I'm a 3D artist and aspiring indie game developer currently studying Animación 3D, Juegos y Entornos Interactivos in Pamplona. My background covers systems administration and web dev — but Blender is where I live: modeling, lighting, shading, compositing.

Before this I completed a Grado Medio in Sistemas Microinformáticos y Redes. Internships in web dev and real-estate video editing taught me that deadlines and client taste are their own creative constraint.

My aim is to ship original indie games where every art direction call, mechanic, and design decision comes from the same pair of hands.
