About

Who I am.

Based in Pamplona, Spain. I'm currently studying Animación 3D, Juegos y Entornos Interactivos at Donapea — building on a background in systems administration and networking. My work lives at the intersection of 3D craft and game development. I spend most of my time in Blender, breaking and rebuilding things until they look right.

I also have hands-on experience in web development and video production from two professional internships, which means I think about the full pipeline, not just the artistic end.

I want to make indie games — not just play them. From the first polygon to the final build, I want full authorship over the thing I'm making. Right now I'm learning everything I can, one project at a time, building the foundation for something bigger.
