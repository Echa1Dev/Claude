01 About

Building worlds,
one render
at a time.

I'm a 3D artist and aspiring indie game developer currently studying Animación 3D, Juegos y Entornos Interactivos in Pamplona. My background covers systems administration and web development — but Blender is where I live: modeling, lighting, shading, compositing.

Before 3D, I completed a Grado Medio in Sistemas Microinformáticos y Redes. Internships in web development and real-estate video editing taught me that deadlines and client taste are a creative constraint of their own.

My aim is to ship original indie games where every art direction call, mechanic, and design decision comes from the same pair of hands.
